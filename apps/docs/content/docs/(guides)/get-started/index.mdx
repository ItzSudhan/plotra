Five steps, about ten minutes. Each one ends with a link to the full story, so you can stop and read more wherever you like.

<StartWriting>Create your free account</StartWriting>

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign up and name your workspace

Sign up with your name, an email address and a password, or with **Continue with Google**. Plotra then asks for a workspace name. A **workspace** is you, or you and your team, and it holds your projects. Press Enter to accept the name it suggests, or type your own. You can rename it later in the workspace's settings.

More: [Your account and workspaces](/docs/settings/account).

</Step>
<Step>

### Start a project

A new account lands on a few short questions: first what you are making, then who the story is about, where they are, and what they hide or want. Each answer becomes a linked note, so the project starts with a scene, a character and a place already joined up. **Skip, just give me a blank page** at the bottom skips them.

Would you rather look around first? Open a [sample project](/docs/get-started/sample-projects) instead: a small finished story with one mistake to find.

More: [Your first project](/docs/get-started/first-project).

</Step>
<Step>

### Write, and link as you go

The project opens on its first scene with the cursor in it. Type. There is no save button: changes are saved as you write, and the status bar says **Saved**.

- Type `[[` and a name to link to another note. The note you link to then lists this scene under **Links** in the inspector, on the right.
- Type `/` for headings, lists, tables, callouts and images.
- Make a new scene with **New** at the top of the binder, on the left, or press `⌘⌥N`.

More: [The editor](/docs/writing/editor), [Links](/docs/writing/links), [The binder](/docs/writing/binder).

</Step>
<Step>

### See it another way

The strip of buttons at the far left is the **ribbon**. Each button opens a view of the same project: the **Graph** of how notes link, the **Codex** of characters and places, the **Outline** of every scene. **More** has every other view: corkboard, plot grid, timeline and the rest. Views open beside your writing as tabs.

More: [A tour of the screen](/docs/get-started/the-screen), [Views](/docs/views).

</Step>
<Step>

### Invite someone

Choose **Share project…** at the top of the binder, type an email address and pick what they may do: **Viewer** reads, **Commenter** reads and comments, **Editor** writes too. Once they are in, you see each other's cursors as you write.

More: [Inviting and sharing](/docs/collaboration/sharing).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Three keys worth learning

| Keys | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `⌘O` | **Find a note**: jump to any note by name |
| `⌘P` | **All commands**: every command, searchable |
| `⌘/` | Every keyboard shortcut |

On Windows and Linux, use `Ctrl` where these docs say `⌘`.
