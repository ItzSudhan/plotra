Plotra keeps your manuscript and the world behind it in one linked project, so you stop hunting through docs, spreadsheets and group chats for a detail, and catch the contradictions before a reader does. Think of Obsidian, built for stories, with more than one person in it: scenes, characters, places, a timeline and a plot grid, written together live. Plotra tracks what is canon, who knows which secret, and what readers know as of each book.

<StartWriting />

The hosted version has a Free plan with every feature, and a Pro plan at $10 a month for the whole workspace, not per person, with more room, more collaborators and hosted AI. See [Plans and billing](/docs/settings/plans).

## What you can do

- **Everything about your story, one click away.** Notes link to each other with `[[wiki-links]]`, so the scene you're in leads to every character, place and secret it mentions. See [Links and backlinks](/docs/writing/links).
- **A new angle when you're stuck.** The same project as prose, a timeline, a storyboard, a corkboard, a plot grid, an outline or a relationship map, side by side. See [Views](/docs/views).
- **One draft, not five copies of it.** Live co-editing with cursors, comments, suggestions and version history. See [Working together](/docs/collaboration).
- **A world that doesn't contradict itself.** Canon status on every note, releases, a spoiler horizon that shows the world as your audience knows it, who-knows-what tracking and continuity checks. See [Canon and the world bible](/docs/canon).
- **Tools for your medium, when you want them.** Script formats, production breakdowns, branching dialogue, translation, research and publishing, each switched on per project. See [Modules](/docs/modules).

## Ask an AI about Plotra

These docs are written to be read by an assistant as well as by you.

- **On any page**, the **Open** menu under the title sends that page to ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor or Scira with a question ready to ask. **Copy Markdown** copies the page so you can paste it anywhere.
- **For the whole docs**, give your assistant [`/llms-full.txt`](/llms-full.txt): every page in one plain-text file. [`/llms.txt`](/llms.txt) is the index.
- **Any page as Markdown**: add `.md` to its address.
