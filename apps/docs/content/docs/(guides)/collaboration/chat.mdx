title: Chat

The **Chat** panel in the inspector is for talking about the work: who's writing which chapter, whether the twist lands, what to do next. Comments are for the words themselves and stay anchored to them; see [Comments and suggestions](/docs/collaboration/comments-and-suggestions).

## The two chats

At the top of the panel, choose which chat you're in:

![The Chat tab of the inspector beside a scene: Project chosen, three messages from Alex Morgan, and the box Message the project…](/shots/collab-chat.webp)

| Chat | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Project** | The whole project's conversation. Everyone who can open the project can read it. |
| **This note** | A conversation about the note you have open, kept with it. The button reads **This note:** and the note's title. Everyone who can open that note can read it. |

The panel remembers which one you chose while you move between notes. **This note** is greyed out when no note is open.

The panel shows the latest 100 messages of the chat, oldest at the top. A chat nobody has written in yet says "Nothing said in this project yet." or "Nothing said about this note yet."

## Writing

Type in the box at the bottom ("Message the project…" or "Message about this note…") and press `Enter` or **Send**; `Shift`+`Enter` starts a new line. Type `@` and the start of a name to mention someone in the project: they get a [notification](/docs/collaboration/notifications) that opens the chat. You can mention workspace members and the people the project is shared with, who are marked **Guest** in the list, as in [comments](/docs/collaboration/comments-and-suggestions#mentions). Mentions are picked out in colour.

Messages from one person less than three minutes apart are shown together under one name. Hover over a message for its exact time.

| Limit | |
| --- | --- |
| Length of a message | 4,000 characters. Anything longer is cut off. |
| Messages you can send | 120 in ten minutes |

Anyone who can comment can write: owners, admins, editors and commenters. Viewers read along, and see "You can read this chat. Commenters and editors can write in it."

## Live

A message appears for everyone who has the chat open as soon as it is sent. When someone writes in a chat while your Chat panel isn't showing, a dot appears on the **Chat** tab until you open it.

## Deleting

Hover over one of your own messages and click the bin to delete it; Plotra asks first, as it goes for everyone. Someone who manages the project can delete anyone's. Messages can't be edited.

Deleting a note deletes its chat with it. A message by someone who is no longer one of the project's people is shown under "Someone".

The chat isn't part of a project's [read-only links](/docs/collaboration/sharing#read-only-links), and isn't copied when a reader [copies the project](/docs/collaboration/sharing#letting-readers-copy-it).

## Customize

The Chat panel is a tab of the inspector like the others: hide it, or move it, in **Customize**. See [Customize](/docs/settings/customize).
