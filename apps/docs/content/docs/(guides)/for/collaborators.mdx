Someone has let you into their project. You read the same draft they are writing, always the latest one, and depending on your role you comment on it, react to it, suggest changes or write in it beside them. No file is sent back and forth.

![A scene with the Comments panel open: one suggestion with Reject and Accept, and one thread that quotes the passage, with a reply and Resolve](/shots/collab-comments.webp)

## Which one are you

| You were | You are a | You can |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invited to a **workspace** | **Member** or **Admin** | Read, comment and edit in every project of that workspace, and create projects. An admin also manages projects and members. |
| Given one **project** as editor | **Editor** | Read, comment and edit in that project |
| Given one project as commenter | **Commenter** (a beta reader) | Read and comment |
| Given one project as viewer | **Viewer** | Read |
| Sent a link that starts with `/p/` | A visitor | Read, without an account |

Someone with a project role who isn't a member of its workspace is a **guest** there. The trail above a note shows **Read only** for a viewer and **Can comment** for a commenter.

## Set up

### Accepting a workspace invitation

1. Open the link in the email, "*Name* invited you to *Workspace* on Plotra". It is good for 48 hours.
2. Sign in, or choose **Create an account** on the sign-in page. You are brought back to the invitation.
3. On the page titled **Join *Workspace***, press **Accept and join**.

You must be signed in with the address the invitation was sent to. Otherwise the page says **Invitation not available**: ask for a new invitation to the address you use. See [Accepting an invitation](/docs/collaboration/sharing#accepting-an-invitation).

### Opening a shared project

1. Open the link in the email, "*Name* shared “*Project*” with you on Plotra".
2. Sign in or create an account with the address it was sent to. The project opens, and from then on the access belongs to your account.

If you see **This was shared with a different address**, sign in with that address or ask for the project to be shared with the one you use. See [Opening a shared project](/docs/collaboration/sharing#opening-a-shared-project).

### Finding it again

Projects from workspaces you don't belong to are on your **Shared with you** page, each with the workspace it lives in and your role. With no workspace of your own, that page is where Plotra opens. If you have one, a **Shared with you** link appears on its home page.

## Typical workflow

1. **Read.** Click a note in the binder, or open the **Read** view to see a folder laid out as pages. Every view is open to you, whatever your role.
2. **Comment on a passage.** Select the text and click **Comment** beside it, or press `⌘⌥M`. Write, then press `⌘↵`. With nothing selected the comment is on the whole note. See [Comments](/docs/collaboration/comments-and-suggestions#comments).
3. **Follow the threads.** Comments are in the inspector under **Review → Comments**. **Reply** adds to a thread, and anyone who can comment can **Resolve** or **Reopen** one.
4. **React.** If the project has the Review & editorial tool on, selecting text also offers six one-click reactions, from "Loved it" to "Bored". Click your own reaction again to take it back. See [Reactions](/docs/modules/editorial#reactions).
5. **Answer a questionnaire.** Open **Reviews** from **More** in the ribbon and go to the **Questionnaires** tab. Press **Answer**, fill in what you want to, and press **Send answers**. You can change your answers while it is open. See [Questionnaires](/docs/modules/editorial#questionnaires).
6. **Suggest a change** (editors and members). Press `⌘⇧E` to turn on suggesting. What you type is marked as an insertion and what you delete as a deletion, until someone who can edit accepts or rejects it. See [Suggestions](/docs/collaboration/comments-and-suggestions#suggestions).
7. **Write beside the author** (editors and members). Open the same note and type. You see each other's cursors, and text from both of you is merged. See [Writing at the same time](/docs/collaboration#writing-at-the-same-time).
8. **Review, if you are asked** (members). A note waiting for your approval is under **Reviews → Inbox → Waiting for you**. Press **Approve**, or **Request changes** and say what needs to change. See [Giving a review](/docs/modules/editorial#giving-a-review).
9. **Stay informed.** The bell lists replies to threads you took part in, and **Your settings** decides what is also emailed. See [Notifications](/docs/collaboration/notifications).

| Read comments and suggestions, compare versions | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Comment, reply, resolve | | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| React, answer a questionnaire | | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Set a line's status on a recording sheet | | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Write, suggest, accept and reject suggestions | | | Yes | Yes |

| Share the project, change its settings | | | | Admins and owners |

The full matrix is on [Roles and permissions](/docs/reference/roles-and-permissions).

## Reading through a read-only link

A link that starts with `/p/` opens a plain reading page without an account: the binder on the left and the selected note on the right. You see every note with its text, tags and properties, and links between notes work. Canvases, timelines and dialogues are listed but not shown. There are no comments, no search and no views, and you can't comment, edit or export. If the link is switched off or reset, it stops working at once. See [A read-only link](/docs/collaboration/sharing#read-only-links).

## Things to know

- **You see the whole project.** There are no permissions on single notes, so a viewer or a beta reader can open everything, including notes the story has not revealed yet. You can set a spoiler horizon for your own view if you would rather not be spoiled.
- **A guest can be mentioned once they have accepted their invite.** Typing `@` lists them marked **Guest**. You are also notified of replies in threads you commented in.
- **Your name may not show to others.** Names come from the workspace's member list, so a guest's comments, reactions and questionnaire answers are shown to other people under "Former member", and a guest's suggestions under "Someone".
- **Questionnaire answers are not anonymous** to the people who can edit the project, and you see only your own.
- **A commenter can't suggest.** Suggesting mode needs edit access. Say what you would change in a comment.
- **Canvases, timelines and dialogues have no comments.**
- **A guest can't remove themselves** from a project: ask an owner or admin. A member can leave a workspace with **Leave this workspace** under **Settings → General**.
- **When access is taken away,** it ends on your next request, and a note you have open stops updating within ten minutes.
- **Your account.** Sign up with the address the invitation went to. Afterwards you can change your name, email and password under **Your settings → Profile and account**, and **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page emails you a link if you lose it. You can have a workspace of your own at the same time.
