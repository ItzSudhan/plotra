Plotra can be used from outside the app: a script that files your research, an automation that drafts notes, or an AI assistant such as Claude or Cursor that reads your world and writes into it. They all sign in with an **API token** you make in your settings, and act as you.

A token can do what you can, in the projects you can open, and nothing more. Every request goes through the same permission checks as the app: a token of a viewer can't edit, and a token can't see a project you weren't let into.
