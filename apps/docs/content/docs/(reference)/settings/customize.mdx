**Customize** is where you set up the frame for yourself. Nothing here changes anyone else's screen, and it follows you to every project and device.

## Opening it

Open it from **More → Customize…** in the ribbon, by right-clicking a ribbon view, or with the command "Customize: presets, ribbon, shortcuts, toolbar and the look of the page". The gear at the right of the inspector's tabs, **Customize the inspector**, opens it too.

![The Customize dialog: a search box, then the Work like… presets, from Plotra and Like Scrivener to Distraction-free, each with a line on what it sets](/shots/customize.webp)

## What you can change

The sections are on four tabs: **Look**, **Layout**, **Keys and typing** and **Advanced**. Type in the search box at the top to find a setting on any of them. It also finds the settings that live elsewhere, in project settings and Your settings, with a link to them.

| Tab | Section | What you can change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Look | **Work like…** | Start from the layout of an app you know: **Like Scrivener**, **Like Plottr**, **Like Google Docs**, **Like Obsidian**, **Like World Anvil or LegendKeeper**, or **Distraction-free**. **Plotra** is how a new project starts. See [Work like another app](#work-like-another-app). |
| Look | **Interface size** | How big the buttons, menus and labels are drawn, on every page: **Compact**, **Comfortable** (the default), **Large** or **Larger**. The text you write has its own size, under **The page**. See [Interface size](#interface-size). |
| Look | **The page** | Typeface, text size, line width, line spacing and paragraphs. See [The page](#the-page). |
| Look | **Colours** | A theme for the app (one of Plotra's, one from a plugin, or one you make, each with a light and a dark version) and an accent colour. See [Colours and your own CSS](#colours-and-your-own-css). |
| Look | **Opening and printing** | **Open projects in focus mode**, **Typewriter scrolling from the start**, and the **Paper** the Read view lays pages out on and prints to: **US Letter** or **A4**. |
| Layout | **Ribbon** | Which views are in the ribbon and in what order, or **Show every view**. See [The ribbon](#the-ribbon). |
| Layout | **Inspector** | Which panels are shown and in what order: Properties, Outline, Links, Story, Comments, History, AI. Panels the project doesn't have are not offered. Tick **One tab for each panel, instead of Note and Review** to stop panels sharing tabs. With everything hidden, Links stays. |
| Layout | **Status bar** | Which items the status bar shows, and their order: **Workspace and project name**, **Spoiler horizon**, **Words in the project**, **Words in this note**, **Characters in this note (with spaces)**, **Words this session**, **Saved or saving** (going offline always shows), **Who else is here** and **Notifications bell**. The workspace and project name, the project's words and the characters start hidden, to keep a first open calm. See [The status bar](/docs/get-started/the-screen#the-status-bar). |
| Layout | **Formatting toolbar** | Which buttons the toolbar over selected text shows, and their order: **AI** (also in the / menu), **Turn into**, **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Strikethrough**, **Highlight**, **Inline code**, **Link**, **Breakdown tag** and **Comment**. **Inline code** starts hidden. Their shortcuts work either way. **AI** shows only where AI is on, and **Breakdown tag** only while the Production tool is on. |
| Layout | **Binder header** | Which buttons the top of the binder shows, and their order: **New** (scenes, chapters, folders and every other type), **More** (import, export and beat sheets), **Collapse all**, **Share project**. What they do stays in **All commands**. |
| Layout | **Note menu (right-click or ⋯)** | What right-clicking a note, or its **⋯** button, offers: opening it, its local graph, a folder's views, **New inside**, **Save as a beat sheet**, **Duplicate**, **Change type**, **Status**, **Import** and **Export**. **Rename** and **Move to Trash** always stay, and so do their keys (`F2`, `Delete`). |
| Layout | **Project home** | Which blocks the project home shows, and their order. |
| Layout | **Guidance** | **Suggest a way in when a note is empty**, and whether the getting-started checklist shows in this project. |
| Keys and typing | **Keyboard shortcuts** | Opens the shortcuts sheet, says how many you have changed, and resets them. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/reference/keyboard-shortcuts#changing-a-shortcut). |
| Keys and typing | **Typing** | **Markdown as you type**, **Smart quotes and dashes**, **Turn web addresses into links**, **A list of notes on [[** and **Check spelling**. See [The editor](/docs/writing/editor#markdown-shortcuts). |
| Keys and typing | **All commands** | Commands to take out of the **All commands** list (`⌘P`), by group. A command taken out still runs from its shortcut. |
| Advanced | **Your own CSS** | For people who know CSS: style rules of your own, applied to every page, for you only. See [Colours and your own CSS](#colours-and-your-own-css). |
| Advanced | **Defaults for others** | **Make mine this project's default** (people who manage the project) and **Make mine the workspace's default** (owners and admins). See [Defaults for a project or a workspace](#defaults-for-a-project-or-a-workspace). |
| Advanced | **Your setup as a file** | **Save to a file** writes all of the above to `plotra-setup.json`; **Load from a file…** puts a saved setup back, yours or someone else's. |
| Advanced | **Start over** | **Reset everything…** puts everything in Customize back to how a new project starts. |

In **Status bar**, **Formatting toolbar** and **Binder header**, drag a row to move it, or use its up and down arrows (they work from the keyboard). A hidden item keeps its place, so it comes back where you put it. The note menu and **All commands** can't be reordered.

Each section has **Reset to default** once you have changed it (for those three, it brings back what you hid and the order they start in). **Reset everything…**, under **Start over**, asks first ("Reset everything?"): your ribbon, theme, custom CSS, shortcuts, toolbar and the look of the page go back to how they start. **Undo** in the message after it puts them back. Your interface size and animations stay as they are.

## Interface size

**Interface size**, at the top of **Look**, makes the whole app bigger or smaller: buttons, menus, labels and the space around them, on every page. Choose **Compact**, **Comfortable**, **Large** or **Larger**. It doesn't change the text you write; that has its own **Text size**, under [The page](#the-page).

It is kept with your account, so it follows you to every device, and a copy is kept on each device so the next page opens at the right size. **Reset to default** goes back to Comfortable.

## The ribbon

The ribbon is yours, not the project's. Your arrangement is saved with your account, so it follows you to every project and every device, and your co-writers keep their own.

A project's ribbon starts with five views, chosen by how the project was started. See [Project types](/docs/reference/project-types#the-ribbon-a-project-starts-with). Every other view is under **More**.

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Pin a view | Open **Customize** and tick it. Finishing a checklist step about a view also pins it. |
| Hide a view | Right-click it in the ribbon and choose **Remove from ribbon**, or untick it in Customize. It moves under **More**. |
| Reorder | Right-click a view and choose **Move up** or **Move down**, or use the arrows in Customize. |
| Show all | In Customize, tick **Show every view**. Pinning, hiding and reordering are switched off while it is on, and the ribbon scrolls if the views don't fit. |
| Start again | **Reset to default**, beside the Ribbon heading in Customize. |

A view that belongs to a tool is only offered while that tool is on.

If you are offline when you change the ribbon, it stays as you arranged it in that tab and is saved with your next change.

## The page

How the text you write looks on screen. Printed and exported pages have their own formats. See also [How the page looks](/docs/writing/editor#how-the-page-looks).

| Setting | Choices (default first) |
| --- | --- |
| **Typeface** | Serif, Sans-serif, Typewriter |
| **Text size** | Medium, Small, Large, Extra large |
| **Line width** | Normal, Narrow, Wide |
| **Line spacing** | Normal, Tight, Relaxed |
| **Paragraphs** | Space between, First line indented |

## Defaults for a project or a workspace

Your setup is yours alone, but you can make it the one everyone else starts from. A default holds the page (typeface, size, line width, line spacing, paragraphs), the inspector's panels, the project home's blocks and the ribbon.

- **Make mine this project's default**, for people who manage the project: everyone in the project who hasn't chosen their own sees yours.
- **Make mine the workspace's default**, for the workspace's owners and admins: the same for every project of the workspace, under any project's own default.
- **Take it away** removes a default; people go back to the next one down.

Someone's own choice always wins: the page goes choice by choice (pick a typeface and the project's text size still applies), the inspector, the home and the ribbon each as a whole once they've arranged them. **Reset to default** in a section of Customize goes back to the project's default, then the workspace's, then Plotra's. A default is copied when it's made: change your setup afterwards and the default stays as it was until you make it again.

## Colours and your own CSS

A theme sets the app's backgrounds, text, borders and, for some, the accent. Each has a light and a dark version, which follow the light and dark switch as before. **Colours** lists three kinds:

- **Plotra's**: **Paper** (Plotra's own), **Sepia**, **Slate**, **Forest**, **Pure**, **High contrast**, **Dusk**, **Fjord**, **Rosewood** and **Mocha**.
- **From your plugins**: themes that [plugins](/docs/developers/plugins#a-theme) add, from the **Themes** shelf of the plugin store or your own. Once picked, a plugin's theme stays in use on every page, even where plugins don't run; it updates when the plugin does.
- **Yours**: themes you make. **New theme** starts one from the theme in use. Give it a name, then pick its six colours for light and for dark: **Background**, **Pages and cards**, **Sidebar**, **Text**, **Quieter text** and **Accent**. The rest (menus, borders, hover, focus rings) follow from those. Changes show at once. A warning appears when text or the accent would be hard to read. You can keep 12.

To share a theme you made, choose **Add as a plugin** (it joins your plugins, ready to [publish to the store](/docs/developers/plugins)) or **Save as a plugin file** to send the `.js` file to someone.

You can also switch themes from your account menu (**Colours**), or with **Use the … theme** in **All commands**.

The accent colour is used for buttons, focus rings, the active tab and highlights: the theme's own where it has one, or **Ember** (Plotra's own), **Gold**, **Green**, **Teal**, **Blue**, **Violet** or **Rose**.

**Your own CSS**, on the **Advanced** tab, applies a stylesheet of yours to every page, for you only. Choose **Apply** to use it. The app's colours are CSS variables, such as `--background`, `--foreground`, `--card`, `--border` and `--ember` (the accent), set on `html:root` for light and `html.dark` for dark. Anything that would load from elsewhere is taken out (`url(…)`, `@import`, `image-set`, and backslashes), so a setup file from someone else can't use CSS to send what's on your screen anywhere. Up to 20,000 characters.

Colours are kept with your account, and a copy on each device so pages open in your colours straight away. In **Colours**, **Reset to default** goes back to Paper and its accent, and keeps the themes you made; **Your own CSS** has its own **Reset to default**, which takes your CSS away.

## Work like another app

A preset sets the ribbon, the inspector, the page, typing, the status bar, the toolbar, the project home and a few shortcuts in one go. Pick one and choose **Apply**. It replaces what you had set in those sections; your animations and the empty-note prompt stay as they are. Everything it sets can be changed afterwards. The preset you started from is marked **Current preset**, and the line above the list names it.

| Preset | What it gives |
| --- | --- |
| **Like Scrivener** | Manuscript, corkboard and outline first, indented paragraphs, and `⌘⌥F` for focus mode, as Scrivener's composition mode. |
| **Like Plottr** | The plot grid, outline, corkboard and relationships first, the manuscript after them. |
| **Like Google Docs** | The manuscript and outline only, comments and history first in the inspector, and a quiet status bar. |
| **Like Obsidian** | The graph, codex and outline first, one tab per inspector panel, and `⌘⇧F` to search the project. Focus mode gives up that key; it stays in **All commands**. |
| **Like World Anvil or LegendKeeper** | The codex, graph, relationships, secrets and continuity first. |
| **Distraction-free** | The manuscript only, a typewriter face in a narrow column, and almost nothing in the status bar or the toolbar. |

A view whose tool is off in this project isn't in the ribbon until the tool is switched on; the preset names those before you apply it.
