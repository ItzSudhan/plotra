## What this is

A fantasy novel for adults, about 90,000 words. In a canal city kept dry by nine bells, a keeper's apprentice finds out that one of them was never cast.

## Voice

Plain and dry. Short sentences when people are lying.

## Tense and point of view

Past tense. Close third person, one point-of-view character per scene.

## Things to avoid

The word "suddenly". Modern slang.
